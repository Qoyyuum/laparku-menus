Every menu in Brunei,
one open dataset.

laparku — from lapar, “hungry” — is a community-built, searchable collection of food & beverage menus and products: restaurants, roadside stalls, supermarkets and grocers. It powers apps, agents and healthier choices through a public API and an MCP endpoint.

What is laparku?

🍽️

Menus & products

Dishes, drinks and grocery items from restaurants, stalls, supermarkets and grocers across Brunei — names, prices and details contributed by the community and kept searchable.

🔌

Open API & MCP

Query the dataset over a public HTTP API, or plug it straight into your AI agent via the MCP endpoint. Build apps, bots and analyses on open data. Connect to the MCP server →

🥗

Healthier eating

A healthy-eating initiative at heart: when what's in your food — and what it costs — is open data, everyone can make better choices.
